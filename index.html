The AI can find information, but the business stays fragmented, and you keep paying for every tool underneath.

  6. Entities, dates and figures, each verified.

  7. A controlled vocabulary, so questions can be scoped.

  8. Meaning and exact match, together.

08 Questions

Frequently asked.

What is Silenus?

An AI-native business operating system by Phannes Inc. Documents, projects, CRM, email, calendar and HR run on one shared knowledge base, and people approve what the AI prepares.

How is Silenus different from an ERP with AI features?

ERPs add an assistant beside separate modules, each with its own database. In Silenus everything enters one knowledge base first and every module is a view of it, so nothing is copied and answers draw on the whole company.

Do we have to replace Microsoft 365 or SharePoint?

No. Keep SharePoint and your mailboxes. Silenus reads from them, checks every answer against the source, and runs the work that follows.

Which tools does Silenus connect to?

Microsoft 365 and Gmail (shared mailboxes too), SharePoint, Google Drive, Google and Outlook calendars, Shopify, and external AI tools through MCP. Bank connections are next on the roadmap.
