How it works

Enter once. Use everywhere.

A complete platform, not a tool on top of your apps. Information enters once; every function works from it.

  1. 01Information enters
  2. 02Understood
  3. 03Connected
  4. 04Put to work

  2. NO—1Information enters

    Your information comes in.

    Documents, emails, scans and spreadsheets enter Silenus once.

    Connecting Microsoft 365, Gmail, SharePoint or Google Drive is optional.

  3. NO—2Understood

    Silenus understands it.

    Agents read every document, scans and tables included, and check each fact against its source page.

    Every fact keeps a link to its source.

  4. NO—3Connected

    Everything gets connected.

    A contract, an invoice and an email about one customer link to one record.

    Recorded once, used everywhere: nothing is copied between modules.

  5. NO—4Put to work

    Teams and AI share one context.

    People decide; AI drafts and flags what is missing. Nothing changes or leaves the company without approval.

    An email is never sent automatically: someone presses Send.
