05 Questions

Frequently asked.

What is Silenus?

An AI-native business operating system by Phannes Inc. Documents, projects, CRM, email, calendar and HR run on one shared knowledge base, and people approve what the AI prepares.

How is Silenus different from an ERP with AI features?

ERPs add an assistant beside separate modules, each with its own database. In Silenus everything enters one knowledge base first and every module is a view of it, so nothing is copied and answers draw on the whole company.

Do we have to replace Microsoft 365 or our other tools?

No. Keep Microsoft 365, SharePoint and your mailboxes: Silenus reads from them, checks every answer against the source, and runs the work that follows. It connects to Microsoft 365 and Gmail (shared mailboxes too), SharePoint, Google Drive, Google and Outlook calendars, Shopify, and external tools through MCP. Bank connections are next on the roadmap.

Do we have to keep files in folders?

No folders to maintain. Every file carries its own metadata and lands in the right place automatically.

Does the AI act on its own?

No. Every tool is classed as read, change or external effect, and changes arrive as approval cards with the exact arguments. An e-mail is never sent automatically: a person presses Send. The AI workforce on the roadmap, AI colleagues with a role, schedule and permissions, works under the same rules, managed next to your human team.

How do we get started?

Book a live test. Bring 30–50 of your own documents and we run the test with you, live. No consulting project to get started, and a private, self-hosted deployment is available.
