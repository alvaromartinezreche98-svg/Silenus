02 Controls

Rules the AI cannot skip.

The same rules for people and for the AI, built into every module.

  • readchangeexternal effectunclassifiedrefused

    Asks before it acts

    Every AI tool is classed as read, change or external effect. An unclassified tool is refused.

  • automationSend

    Sending stays human

    No automation or standing approval can send an e-mail. A person presses Send.

  • no copy kept

    Mail without a copy

    Mail is read live from Microsoft 365 or Gmail with the user’s own rights. Silenus keeps no copy.

  • isolated framescriptsoff

    Safe reading

    Every message opens in its own isolated frame, without scripts.

  • 24 modulesscopes · actions

    One permission matrix

    24 modules, each with scopes and actions. Anything not granted is refused, for people and for the AI.

  • legal hold

    Retention and legal hold

    Retention policies, legal holds nothing can override, and data-deletion requests.

  • audit_log.csv

    A compliance record

    Audit log with export, a data inventory, and which AI providers received company data.

  • Self-hosted · chat1,240 tokens€0.00OpenAI · project3,860 tokens€0.02Claude · source2,110 tokens€0.01

    AI cost on record

    Every AI call is logged with its tokens and price, by model, chat, project and source.
