ALLLAron & Laura, co-founders

We grew up in our family business and kept hitting the same wall. Our AI tools could help with one file, but nothing could take the whole company's documents, process them at scale and turn them into knowledge we could use.

Silenus started as that missing processing layer and grew into an operating system for the whole company.
