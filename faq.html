The basics

01

What is Silenus?

An AI-native business operating system by Phannes Inc. Documents, projects, CRM, email, calendar and HR run on one shared knowledge base, and people approve what the AI prepares.

02

How is Silenus different from an ERP with AI features?

ERPs add an assistant beside separate modules, each with its own database. In Silenus everything enters one knowledge base first and every module is a view of it, so nothing is copied and answers draw on the whole company.

03

What is included?

Documents, CRM, sales and commerce (Shopify, inventory, quotes), projects, worksheets, templates, team chat, e-mail in your own tone of voice, AI meeting summaries, calendar and the full HR module. Finance and banking are next: invoicing, bank integration, accounting, commissions and a cash-flow forecast.

04

What about accounting?

Coming next: books without a close. Every event is posted the moment it happens (outgoing invoices on issue, incoming invoices on receipt, bank items when matched), so the period can be closed at any minute.

Your tools and files

05

Do we have to replace Microsoft 365 or our other tools?

No. Keep Microsoft 365, SharePoint and your mailboxes: Silenus reads from them, checks every answer against the source, and runs the work that follows.

06

Which tools does Silenus connect to?

Microsoft 365 and Gmail (shared mailboxes too), SharePoint, Google Drive, Google and Outlook calendars and Shopify. External tools connect through MCP, each call approved by a person, and Silenus also serves its own tools to other AI clients. Bank connections are next on the roadmap.

07

Do we have to keep files in folders?

No folders to maintain. Every file carries its own metadata and lands in the right place automatically.

08

Which documents can it read?

28 file types, scans included: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and e-mail, read by 14 specialised agents.

09

What does the HR module include?

Every decision on one screen: who is here, who is in the pipeline and what needs your decision, with compliance signals before they become problems. HR dashboard, Employee dashboard (new), Employees by department, Profiles & onboarding, Organisation chart, Recruitment channels, Performance, HR assistant, Time tracking, My time (new), Attendance, Shift management (new), HR letters (new) and HR analytics (new).

The AI

10

How do I know an answer is right?

Every answer cites the passage it came from, down to the page and clause. Ask for “all of them” and you get the complete list. When your documents don't support an answer, Silenus says so instead of guessing.

11

How much can the AI do?

One agent with 118 tools works across every module: Projects 34, CRM 20, Documents 14, E-mail 8, Team chat 5, HR 4 (read-only) and Calendar 3. It searches, reads the passages around a match and cites every claim.

12

Does the AI act on its own?

No. Every tool is classed as read, change or external effect, and changes arrive as approval cards with the exact arguments. An e-mail is never sent automatically: a person presses Send.

13

Which AI model does it use?

Your choice: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Azure OpenAI, DeepSeek or a self-hosted model, set per company, per user or per chat.

14

What is the AI workforce?

AI colleagues with a role, a schedule and permissions, managed next to your human team. It is on the roadmap, and it works under the same rules as the AI today: people approve every change.

Data and security

15

Can Silenus run on our own servers?

Yes. Silenus can run entirely on your own infrastructure, AI models included. You can also choose the AI provider and model per company, user or conversation. An append-only audit trail records which model produced each answer and whether any data left your environment.

Pricing and getting started

16

How is Silenus priced?

By the information your company processes, not by users or modules. Every package includes all modules, AI and unlimited users up to 500. At 100 people it comes to €872 a month.

17

What does it take to get started?

No consulting project. Connect your sources and the views work straight away. If you want proof first, bring 30 to 50 of your own documents and we'll run a live test with you.
