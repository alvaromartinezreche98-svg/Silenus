01 The difference

Apps organise your business. Silenus understands it.

Others sync separate apps or bolt AI on top. Silenus builds one knowledge layer first, and every module is a view of it.

01 · Bundled apps

Separate apps, kept in sync

Each app keeps its own copy of a customer. Data travels between pairs of apps, and things fall through the gaps.

  • One customer, four copies
  • Every new module is a new integration
  • AI added later, paid in credits

02 · AI on top

An AI layer over existing tools

The AI can find information, but the business stays fragmented, and you keep paying for every tool underneath.

  • Search, but no shared operations
  • Sees only what each tool hands it
  • Every tool still billed underneath

03 · Silenus

One knowledge layer, modules on top

Everything goes into one knowledge base first. Every module reads the same facts, so nothing is copied and nothing gets out of step.

  • One customer, one record
  • A new module is a new view
  • AI built in, included

  • Facts, not just text

    Silenus reads 100k+ pages and extracts parties, dates, amounts and clauses, then links them to clients and projects.

  • Understands by meaning

    It finds “unpaid bill” when the document says “overdue invoice”. Similar meanings sit close together.

  • No folders to maintain

    Every file carries its own metadata and lands in the right place automatically.
